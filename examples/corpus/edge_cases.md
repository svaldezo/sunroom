## Very long paragraph

Density separation works because carbonization removes the cellular water and much of the structural mass from plant tissue while leaving the carbon skeleton intact, and that skeleton has a bulk density lower than water, whereas the mineral component of archaeological sediment consists largely of quartz and clay minerals with bulk densities well above water, so when the two are agitated together in a water column the charred fraction rises and the mineral fraction settles, and the separation is sharp enough that a competent operator can recover the great majority of the carbonized assemblage in a single pass provided the agitation is gentle enough that turbulence does not mechanically drive light particles downward past the point at which buoyancy can return them to the surface before the skimming pass is completed.
