Heritage Tourism Economic Brief 2025

Heritage tourism generated an estimated 3.2 billion dollars in direct visitor spending across the twelve surveyed states in 2025. That figure represents an increase of 11 percent over 2024.

Visitation

Total visitation to interpreted heritage sites reached 41.7 million visits. Median dwell time was 94 minutes, down from 112 minutes in 2019.

Sites offering guided interpretation retained visitors 38 percent longer than sites relying on static signage alone.

Staffing

Median interpretive staff per site fell to 2.4 full-time equivalents. Seasonal staffing now accounts for 61 percent of all interpretive labor hours.

Because seasonal staff turn over annually, institutional knowledge about a site does not accumulate. Sites reported spending an average of 47 hours per season on retraining.

Accessibility

Only 34 percent of surveyed sites met WCAG 2.1 AA standards for their digital interpretive material. Compliance was strongly correlated with annual budget above 500,000 dollars.

Captioning coverage for site-produced video averaged 52 percent. Alt text coverage for interpretive images averaged 19 percent.
